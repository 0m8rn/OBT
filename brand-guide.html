Brand identity

Technical services & applied AI.

01 / The mark

An angular O: an open centre held by a continuous, eight-sided band. Precise geometry gives the identity a technical character without relying on a chip, a robot or a literal gemstone.

Use the primary gold-and-white lockup on black. Use one-colour versions on light backgrounds, small prints and restricted-colour applications.

03 / Typography

Technology that works.
Room to go further.

Manrope for website headings and body text. DM Mono for labels, numbers and technical details. System fonts remain the fallback. The logo is supplied as outlined vector artwork: no font installation required.

04 / Voice

Clear. Capable. Grounded.

  • Lead with the problem solved and the next step.
  • Explain scope and price before work begins.
  • Describe practical automation, not invented AI capabilities.
  • Avoid “world-leading”, “revolutionary” and unverified performance claims.

05 / Usage

  • Always write the English brand name as OBSIDIA. Arabic brand spelling: أوبسيديا.
  • Keep clear space around the logo at least half the symbol's height.
  • Recommended minimums: full lockup 160 px wide; symbol 24 px. Use the favicon asset for smaller browser icons.
  • Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows or change the logo's proportions.
  • Use the square avatar for WhatsApp and social profiles; it has space for circular cropping.
